PRE-DEPARTURE CHECKLIST:
  1. Vehicle brakes checked and working properly

  3. Tank integrity verified - no leaks or damage
  4. Fire extinguisher and emergency spill kit present
  5. Mobile phone fully charged with all emergency numbers saved
  6. ADR certification and transport documents verified
  7. Weather forecast checked - safe to travel
  8. All danger zone GPS coordinates noted
DRIVING SAFETY RULES:
  1. Speed Limits: Maximum {{ tt_specs.max_speed if tt_specs and tt_specs.max_speed else '50' }} km/h highway, 30 km/h city, 10-25 km/h at danger zones
  2. Following Distance: Maintain 6-second gap from vehicle ahead
  3. Braking: Use engine braking on all slopes and before turns - avoid sudden foot brake
  4. Liquid Surge: Monitor constantly - sudden braking can cause rollover
  5. No Overtaking: On curves, turns, uphill sections, or narrow roads
  6. Rest Breaks: Every 2 hours for minimum 15 minutes
  7. Weather: Avoid travel in heavy rain, fog, or adverse conditions
  8. Position Reporting: Report to VTS every 30 minutes
EMERGENCY PROTOCOL

In case of accident, spillage, or emergency:
1. Stop vehicle immediately in safe location
2. Turn on hazard lights
3. Call 101 (Fire), 100 (Police), or 108 (Medical) as needed
4. Call IOCL Emergency: 1800-2333-555
5. Inform VTS Coordinator immediately
6. Do not leave accident site until authorities arrive
